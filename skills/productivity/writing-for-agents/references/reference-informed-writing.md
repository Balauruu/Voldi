# Auditing and evaluating reference-informed writing

Read this before comparing learned writing guidance with an actual generated script or designing a later trial. Use the audit procedure to locate supported loss points without rewriting the example or inferring hidden worker reasoning. Use the evaluation-design procedure to turn those findings and confirmed corrections into an owner-approved later exercise, not to execute it.

## Procedure

1. **Establish the actual comparison.** Resolve the complete reference collection, preparation notes, analyses, synthesis, exported guides, current demonstration inputs, initial plan, final prose and available review context. Separate current files from historical execution claims. Check that copied guides/core/material are the intended versions, inspecting their relevant content and using a direct comparison when unmodified copying matters to the exercise; agreement establishes present contents, not historical consumption. Keep read-only audits read-only. Use initial/final Git status and focused diffs where they help distinguish relevant changes; reserve targeted no-change comparisons for an expressly protected artifact or a concrete risk. Report the inspected scope and observation window, not a blanket unchanged-repository claim or a repository-wide hash baseline. If a concurrent edit appears, inspect its relevance, preserve it and qualify the affected claim without attributing its author or treating it as historical input.
2. **Inspect complete sources before extracting traits.** Account for narrator speech, quotations, other speakers and promotion. Select situated examples with source titles and original timestamps or distinctive wording. Distinguish observable choices from inferred effects and owner preferences. Treat recognition errors and reconstructed formatting as evidence limits, not stylistic habits. Before accepting source-grounded analyses or guides, use the quotation-verification recipe below; exact correspondence checks do not replace interpretation of neighboring context.
3. **Trace a function through every handoff.** For each relevant behavior, inspect its source context, analyst observation, tentative-transfer choice and final shortlist, synthesized operation, exported example/check, plan treatment and actual prose. Record where it remains recognizable, changes purpose, becomes optional or disappears. Distinguish absent extraction, selection loss, recognized-but-weakened synthesis and consumer application; these call for different interventions. Separate a technique's explanatory function, rhetorical packaging and argumentative validity before selecting or declining it, because removing insults can preserve useful reasoning but cannot repair an invalid inference. For familiar-case comparisons, identify the actual disputed rule, its domain and scope, the relevant relation, the comparison's limit and the return to actual subject evidence. Do not widen an object-specific impossibility claim into a universal eyewitness-only rule to make an unrelated case refute it; a supported event concerning another object is not proof of the disputed event's occurrence or possibility. A counterexample to a genuinely general eyewitness-only rule still does not prove that a particular unwitnessed event occurred; where observation was expected, absence may itself require evaluation.
4. **Separate kinds of narrator presence.** Compare audience guidance, narrator-owned judgments, interests or treatment boundaries, and claims about actual investigation or experience. Look for their rhetorical jobs rather than merely counting first-person words. Preserve source-speaker ownership: a quoted witness's first person is not the script narrator's presence. Require an attributable basis for research-process or lived-experience narration; do not transfer another author's experience to make a script feel personal.
5. **Inspect fit to the medium and the supplied material.** Compare whether the script develops an authored inquiry for its audience or largely follows the dossier's order, vocabulary and precomposed conclusions. Similar order alone is not a defect; test whether the sequence advances the live question or repeatedly resets around source introductions. Examine the opening's promise, concrete development, prerequisite ordering, emphasis, transitions, recurrence and earned ending. Before requesting fuller development, inspect whether supplied material contains the actions/reasons needed or only their interpreted summary; narrow an example's job rather than demand invented detail. Separate topic-critical source mediation, consequential inference limits and acquisition housekeeping; preserve necessary uncertainty while converting authoring safeguards into listener-facing explanation. Phrase cumulative genre judgments as supported editorial concerns, not measured audience effects.
6. **Audit affirmative guidance against repair pressure.** Compare permissions with explicit selection conditions, examples and removal rules. A behavior can be allowed yet systematically discouraged by stronger third-person fallbacks, anti-commentary rules or a source-centered plan. Identify these as competing incentives unless actual execution context proves their causal role. Specify the behavior to preserve and its fitting conditions rather than adding a mandatory ingredient list.
7. **Return decision-ready findings.** For each substantive issue give the recognizable passage, observed mismatch, earliest supported stage, competing explanation, consequence and proportionate options. Classify contract gaps, authority inconsistencies, editorial concerns and untested opportunities separately; a legitimate alternative or optional missing example is not automatically a defect. Ask about the unresolved means of satisfying a settled owner requirement, not whether to relax that requirement. Separate existing instruction corrections from candidate new resources/features. When exploration is requested, seek additional gaps beyond the supplied examples; external script-craft research can inform options without selecting them for the owner. Keep implementation and a new writing trial separate when the agreed destination is a corrective specification.

## Verify quotations without confusing their roles

1. Read the complete artifact and its declared evidence sources. Identify which spans claim to reproduce a source, which merely name an analytical concept, and which belong to labeled original applications or misuse examples. Use the governing source representation: when clean text is the analyst's evidence, compare against that clean text rather than reinstating raw-caption wording or timestamps.
2. Extract candidate quotations across the complete artifact with a small transient Python probe, then classify them from their headings, labels and surrounding claims. Include attributed block quotations, inline echoes, nested excerpts and quotations in final selections or summaries; an evidence-block-only or per-title-section check misses later quotation defects. Bind collection-level echoes to their actual title rather than the nearest section heading. Keep analytical labels and labeled original applications separate from attributed evidence, while still checking any source excerpt repeated inside an application. A regular expression over quotation marks is a discovery aid, not a semantic assertion that every result came from a source.
3. Bind each attributed excerpt occurrence to its expected source title, then compare it literally with that source and inspect the neighboring passage for speaker ownership, qualifications and explanatory role. For evidence blocks spanning multiple sources, track explicit title switches in prose as well as colon-delimited labels; keep the title, artifact location and excerpt together in each probe result. Inspect failed bindings against the complete evidence sentence before changing source-grounded prose. Correct a demonstrated locator error and rerun the same literal comparison without dropping the excerpt or checking membership across all sources. Do not accept membership anywhere in the collection as attribution verification: short or shared phrases can match the wrong title. Preserve source capitalization and punctuation; place editorial commas or periods outside excerpt delimiters when the excerpt ends before the source's punctuation. Label paraphrase or an explicitly marked omission instead of silently relaxing equality. Inspect every mismatch before excluding it: a genuinely analytical label or hypothetical application may be excluded when its role is clear, while a purported source excerpt needs correction.
4. Report the checked population precisely: attributed source-excerpt occurrences, analytical/example exclusions and unresolved mismatches separately; state how repeated or nested excerpts are counted. Compute counts from the classified spans rather than subtracting unexplained exceptions. Finish corrections before dependent dispatch, then recheck the current artifact spans against their bound sources using the classified inventory; do not report a corrected inventory entry as passing without confirming the repaired quotation actually appears in the artifact. Preserve a retained pre-repair audit as historical evidence and record the repair/check separately. Inspect repaired prose and neighboring source context for preserved meaning; a passing substring check establishes correspondence, not completeness, interpretation, transfer quality or audience benefit.

## Design a later evaluation

1. **Inspect before choosing inputs.** Read the complete baseline, actual reference passages and confirmed correction boundaries before asking the owner to select a trial. Check whether supplied examples contain actions, reasons and outcomes or only a summarized interpretation; instructions cannot create missing factual detail. Recommend same-material comparison when interpretability is the priority, or a different topic when broader application is the priority. State the tradeoff: one collection/topic cannot establish cross-niche transfer.
2. **Settle the comparison and extent.** Confirm the actual reference collection, topic, complete material, supported length/coverage and comparison scope. For representation comparisons, hold the factual premises, substantive construction obligations, role access, core and outer output contracts constant; vary how the instructions teach the decisions. Do not weaken the control's alternatives or factual safeguards to produce a contrast. Inspect the actual outputs for convergence and report it honestly rather than infer a difference from representation labels. Map the available guide teaching against the shared core and explicit packet cues, then trace what each return actually enacts; a requested operation already taught by the core cannot establish a guide-specific effect. Independent extraction/integration choices and one consumer per packet/variant also limit causal attribution. Distinguish a fresh combined journey from controlled variants that isolate individual changes; the former can reveal preserved/lost functions and editorial differences, not causal attribution. If retaining acquired raw text would hold source content constant, propose it explicitly and obtain confirmation rather than silently replacing a requested acquisition exercise. Name the provenance and label what is not reacquired or independently verified. Keep trial-specific length aims conditional on support, not padding or framework defaults.
3. **Keep diagnostic answers outside creative contexts.** Give fresh analysts and synthesis their actual source inputs, instructions and controls—not old analyses, methods, guides or desired audit conclusions. Give independent writers/reviewers only the declared portable resources, factual material and phase inputs—not the baseline plan/prose or factory reasoning. Compare those artifacts afterward in the coordinator's evaluation; revealing the expected repair can turn a transfer test into fixture imitation. Deliver factual restrictions and current owner direction plainly without prescribing the old audit's preferred output. Preserve role-specific access boundaries rather than extend an analyst-only restriction to synthesis or evaluation.
4. **Specify observable seams and preservation.** Inspect complete preparation separately from word correspondence or verified speech; trace source → observation → tentative transfer/selection → method → guide condition/action/example/check → independent plan/prose. Pair separate voice review of sustained expression/relationship with structure review of supported development, dependencies, progression and closure. Locate cumulative concerns across passages and retain fitting alternatives; neither role certifies extraction from unseen sources. Name the protected inputs and permitted edits at trial start, after any separately authorized baseline reconciliation; verify the relevant preservation boundary with focused inspection rather than inherit stale comparison evidence. Apply the existing frozen-plan and scoped-recheck requirements without adding a reviewer stage, snapshot system or acceptance certificate.
5. **Confirm judgment and stopping before execution.** Ask the owner what recognizable transfer, supported audience-facing inquiry, developed examples, preserved limits and useful workflow would make the actual result acceptable. Restate concrete input/output homes, context isolation, review responsibilities, comparison limits and preservation controls for confirmation. Keep warranted corrections within the existing pass limit; require a later scoped edit only when expressly selected or actually requested, because forced revisions manufacture activity rather than evidence. Deliver final guides, full plan/visual, complete prose and substantive usage/comparison evidence before seeking actual output acceptance. Design approval is not future-output acceptance, and baseline victory, quotas, aggregate scores or retention promises are not default success criteria.

## Render and inspect an embedded plan map

Use when a writing exercise requires a readable rendered copy of the actual plan's Mermaid. Keep the renderer a temporary verification aid, separate from creative instructions and product machinery.

1. **Bind the actual source.** Read the complete plan and extract its fenced `mermaid` block, for example with `re.findall(r'```mermaid\s*\n(.*?)```', text, re.S)`. Verify the expected block population before rendering; do not substitute a coordinator-redrawn diagram. Leave the plan untouched while rendering; a focused before/after text comparison is sufficient when preservation needs checking.
2. **Render on an isolated page.** Use an available capable renderer. A browser path exercised successfully is importing `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/mermaid@11/dist/mermaid.esm.min.mjs`, then calling `initialize({startOnLoad:false, securityLevel:'strict', theme:'default', flowchart:{htmlLabels:true,useMaxWidth:false}})` and `await render(uniqueName, actualSource)`. Load the renderer code without sending the plan to a remote rendering service. When reusing a page, clear its unrelated content and stylesheets before applying explicit document/body sizing and margins: inherited layout rules can shift or constrain the SVG even after replacing the body content. Preserve styles needed by the rendered SVG.
3. **Measure after final layout.** Wait for rendering to finish, inspect the SVG's `getBoundingClientRect()` and node/relationship text, then set the capture viewport with `Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride`. Re-measure after resizing: text wrapping can change the document height, so pre-resize dimensions alone cannot guarantee a complete capture. Require the title, legend, SVG and labels to fit inside the final viewport with clearance; count logical relationships rather than duplicated label-wrapper DOM elements.
4. **Capture and inspect the deliverable.** Save the actual `Page.captureScreenshot` PNG inside the authorized exercise workspace and visually inspect that saved file for readable labels, clipping, collisions, arrow direction and agreement with every planned major section and relationship. Repair rendering/layout defects without silently changing the plan. Deliver the full plan and verified rendered file through the owner's surface; report diagram agreement separately from prose quality or owner acceptance.

## Construct authored transfer controls

Use this recipe when the agreed exercise permits new illustrative reference scripts or supplied-material controls. These controls diagnose a bounded instruction seam; they do not replace specified real inputs or establish general transfer.

1. **Declare provenance and the complete population.** Label each authored reference and material packet as fictional/test-only, give its concrete project-local path, and state which complete texts constitute the exercise. For already readable untimed text, reuse the supplied text unchanged when no preparation repair is needed; record that no repair was needed rather than manufacture acquisition defects, timestamps, parity tables or external verification.
2. **Keep diagnosis out of evidence inputs.** Put expected findings and the coordinator's evaluation criteria outside the reference scripts and analyst assignments. Include situated source behavior, meaningful alternatives and qualifications in the scripts themselves; preserve quoted-speaker ownership. Deliver only the permitted clean texts to isolated analysts, then inspect their actual observation, tentative-transfer and final-selection choices before synthesis. Bind dependent work to the final accepted upstream artifacts rather than previewing the intended guide or answer.
3. **Exercise fitting use and legitimate decline.** Supply separate complete new-topic material for a supported application and an unsuitable-comparison control. In the supported case, provide the actual disputed claim, the relevant everyday relation and its factual limits without supplying finished narration. In the control, make the proposed comparison explicitly optional and preserve the conditions showing why its relation is irrelevant or unsupported. Use the same final guide pair for both; do not require a comparison merely to prove that the kit contains the capacity. Keep source-topic facts and invented narrator experience outside the new-topic factual boundary.
4. **Report what actually happened.** Inspect the real plans, prose and consequential worker returns, including any warranted narrowing or decline. Verify frozen-plan preservation at the actual phase boundaries and obtain the required independent reviews. Reconcile intended construction targets with actual independent findings: if reviewers retain a passage as adequately developed, do not count its intended overcompression as detected or repaired, because fixture intent does not establish an observed defect. Retain that contrary finding and correct only warranted concerns; if a separately required seam remains unexercised, expose the gap rather than silently substitute another success. Distinguish created fixture inputs, dispatched work, inspected outputs and completed behavior; a planned control or a worker still running is not exercised evidence. State the fixture's built-in support and limitations alongside the result, because a supplied comparison premise tests conditional application more directly than spontaneous discovery.

   For an authorized stopping-boundary control, distinguish a clearly labeled hypothetical prior-pass state from the artifact's actual revision history. Supply otherwise complete real inputs, inspect the actual refusal/residual return and verify the protected files remain unchanged; this exercises response to the supplied boundary, not the occurrence of those hypothetical passes. Do not perform pointless revisions to manufacture the test premise.

## Exercise a required repair with a seeded defect

Use only when the agreed exercise requires an actual correction, the complete natural output has no matching defect, and labeled test controls are authorized. Preserve the successful natural result's evidence; do not introduce a flaw merely to consume a correction pass.

1. **Inspect and declare the control.** Establish that the natural output lacks the target defect. State the test-only provenance, exact current artifact, uniquely located old/new span, protected surrounding meaning and frozen inputs before dispatch. Assign only the bounded fixture edit to the artifact's authorized edit owner; this setup assignment is neither an actual owner revision request nor an automatic corrective pass.
2. **Verify the seed independently.** Inspect the actual diff and surrounding text using **Verify scoped prose repairs** below. Confirm that the declared defect was introduced without unrelated edits or changes to frozen inputs before reviewers consume the seeded artifact; a worker's small-change summary does not establish isolation of the defect. For a fixture expressly defined by a literal replacement, a targeted comparison can verify that replacement without making full-file byte reconstruction a general editorial requirement.
3. **Exercise the real repair path.** Use actual independent reviewers and their substantive returns, then the normal integrated writer correction and affected rechecks. Keep suggested wording optional; evaluate the explanatory job and retained factual boundary, not merely disappearance of the seeded phrase. Stop when useful scoped work is exhausted rather than force another pass.
4. **Report the evidence's reach.** Separate natural-draft behavior, fixture setup, actual corrective passes, scoped rechecks and residual advice. State whether reviewers knew the target passage or expected defect; targeted detection/repair is not blind discovery. Preserve broader out-of-scope advice without claiming it resolved, and never generalize the control into defect-frequency, transfer-quality or owner-acceptance evidence.

## Verify scoped prose repairs

Use this recipe when an assignment expressly limits which prose may change; structural work and whole-script repair may need a different permitted region. Preserve editorial freedom within that region rather than require a coordinator-predicted final wording.

1. **Establish the actual boundary before editing.** Read the current canonical prose and frozen plan; retain transient comparison text only where needed to inspect changes. Deliver complete revision context, both actual review returns and completed-pass count for an automatic correction, or the exact labeled scoped request/control. Trace the shared relationship across the affected passages before choosing repair scope. Permit local expression repairs when they resolve that interaction; require rearrangement only for an actual progression or dependency defect, because cross-passage analysis does not itself establish a structural problem. Enumerate permitted passages and preserved meaning; do not substitute a coordinator's predicted review.
2. **Inspect the actual changes against the boundary.** Read a focused text diff alongside the changed passages and their surrounding dependencies. Check that edits stay within the permitted region and retain protected facts, attribution, quotations and accepted meaning. For structural work, inspect the resulting order, development and hinges; for consolidation or deletion, map the original passages to their current locations. Report changed original passages separately from resulting current passages when numbering shifts, and give recheck workers usable current locators. A narrow-looking diff is not sufficient without substantive inspection. Do not reconstruct every permitted byte or reject harmless formatting solely for failing identity unless literal preservation was expressly required.
3. **Check the resulting prose and protected inputs.** Read the complete current prose to check inference, attribution, qualifications, coherence and surrounding dependencies. Confirm the frozen plan was not revised and supplied material/guides remained the intended inputs, using targeted comparisons where needed. If the assignment expressly requires an exact replacement or immutable raw text, verify that specific literal constraint with the simplest direct comparison; use a hash only when it serves a concrete integrity or identification need. Those checks establish literal preservation, not editorial adequacy, and do not expand into whole-repository checkpoints or mandatory hash reporting.
4. **Obtain the affected independent recheck.** Supply the actual current prose, previous concerns, actual change scope and consequential writer declines. Use both separate responsibilities when the change affects expression and progression. Account for the located concerns and any new surrounding effect; describe this as a scoped recheck unless the assignment actually requested and performed a fresh complete review.
5. **Close on the current revision.** Recompute any reported word count from the final file using a stated counting convention. Count only warranted automatic writer revisions after integrated paired-review advice against the agreed automatic-pass bound; reviewer calls, unchanged inspections and separately authorized later scoped owner requests do not consume that allowance. Treat internal polishing before the initial draft handoff as drafting, not another automatic correction cycle, because file-write counts do not identify editorial phase boundaries. Stop when no useful correction remains instead of forcing another pass. Deliver the current artifacts and record preservation, exercised behavior, residual concerns and owner acceptance separately.

## Evidence boundaries

- Check acquired-text correspondence, readability and independently verified spoken wording as distinct properties under the current preparation contract; success at one does not prove the others. If contextual reconstruction is authorized, evaluate full coverage, meaning/order, readability and preservation without reimposing word identity, listening, correction disclosure or owner adjudication as completion gates. Preserve unchanged raw evidence and narrator claims; do not substitute external factual corrections. Treat genuinely unavailable complete input separately from textual ambiguity, because editorial permission cannot supply missing source content.
- Preserve meaningful alternatives and local preferences. A narrator's refusal to use one treatment does not establish a universal ban on difficult material.
- Ground recommendations in the actual conditions of the passage. A small reference collection, correct file transfer or successful trial cannot certify a general voice, retention gain or universal quality improvement.
